          The OSU MGR is funded by NSF to provide curation for marine geological
          samples. Curation and sample dissemination is therefore a central part
          of the OSU MGR's activities. The core lab is equipped with, and has
          access to a range of equipment that can be used to measure cores.


          Some of this is included under standard curation practice, others
          require fees. To use our equipment and/or instrumentation listed
          below, please submit an [Analytical Request](/analytical-requests).
